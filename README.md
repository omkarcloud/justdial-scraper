# 📇 Justdial Scraper

Justdial Scraper is a **free and open-source** scraper that gets you **unlimited** detailed Justdial business data for free.

## ✨ What Can I Get?

- 📇 **Full profiles of 56M+ Indian businesses** — phone, email, website, address, hours, GST & amenities
- 🔍 **Search any category in any Indian city** — up to 100 businesses per search, sorted by rating or popularity
- 📞 **Phone numbers, even the hidden ones** — the number Justdial keeps behind "Show Number"
- ⭐ **Every review & photo** — full review text, owner replies, and every photo by tab

All 6 endpoints are now live at `http://localhost:8000`.

Running on a cloud server? Justdial refuses cloud IPs, so set `JUSTDIAL_PROXY` to a residential proxy (`http://user:pass@host:port`). At home or in the office, you need nothing.

## 📚 Endpoints

6 endpoints cover everything you need.

| Endpoint | Path | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Company Details | `/companies/details` | Full profile: contacts, address, hours, GST, services, photos, reviews |
| Suggest Categories | `/categories/suggest` | Matching categories and businesses with IDs, ratings, listing counts |
| Search Companies | `/companies/search` | 10 businesses per page with phone, rating, address and hours |
| Company Phone | `/companies/phone` | The phone number, even the one behind "Show Number" |
| Company Reviews | `/companies/reviews` | 10 reviews per page with text, rating, tags, owner replies |
| Company Photos | `/companies/photos` | Every photo and video, grouped by tab, with upload dates |
